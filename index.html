02 / How we design

Four rules your customers can feel.

Most AI-made flyers and pages look the same: everything shouting, six fonts, grime on every surface, faces that aren't quite right. Every Sogilex tool is built to the opposite rules.

  1. 01

    One message

    A clear first, second and third thing to read. Never a wall of callouts.

  2. 02

    Two typefaces

    At most. Hierarchy comes from size and space, not from mixing styles.

  3. 03

    No AI look

    No glossy stock art, grime filters or faces that aren't quite right. Clean shapes, real type, and your own photos.

  4. 04

    Only your facts

    No made-up prices, reviews or awards. Anything missing becomes a clear [PLACEHOLDER].

03 / How Build works

Describe it. Change it by asking. Put it online.

  1. Describe your business

    A few sentences, your logo and your own photos. Build designs a one-page site in about two minutes.

  2. Change it by asking

    Type what you want in plain words ("make the buttons green", "add my new photos"), or tap a quick change like "Serif fonts" or "Darker look". Every change is saved, and undo is one click.

  3. Put it online

    Download it as one file to host anywhere, or publish it at your-name.sogilex.net with a working contact form (opening soon).
