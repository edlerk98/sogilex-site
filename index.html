02 / How we design

Four rules your customers can feel.

Most AI-made flyers and pages look the same: everything shouting, six fonts, grime on every surface, faces that aren't quite right. Every Sogilex tool is built to the opposite rules.

  1. 01

    One message

    A clear first, second and third thing to read. Never a wall of callouts.

  2. 02

    Two typefaces

    At most. Hierarchy comes from size and space, not mixing styles.

  3. 03

    No AI look

    No glossy stock art or uncanny faces. Clean shapes, real type, your own photos.

  4. 04

    Only your facts

    No made-up prices, reviews or awards. Missing facts become a clear [PLACEHOLDER].
