02 / How we design

Four rules your customers can feel.

Most AI-made flyers and pages look the same: everything shouting, six fonts, grime on every surface, faces that aren't quite right. Every Sogilex tool is built to the opposite rules.

  1. 01

    One message

    A clear first, second and third thing to read. Never a wall of callouts.

  2. 02

    Two typefaces

    At most. Hierarchy comes from size and space, not from mixing styles.

  3. 03

    Nothing generated to fake it

    No generated people, props or textures. Shapes, type and your own photos only.

  4. 04

    Only your facts

    No made-up prices, reviews or awards. Anything missing becomes a clear [PLACEHOLDER].
