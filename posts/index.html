01 / Made for

The posts a local business actually needs.

  • Deals and specials

    Happy hour, this week's special, a seasonal tune-up price. The deal is the biggest thing on the image.

  • Featured items

    A new dish or product with its photo and price. Restaurants can pick it straight from their menu, price and all.

  • Events and announcements

    Live music, a grand opening, holiday hours, a new location.

  • Hiring and reviews

    "Now hiring" posts, and real reviews from your customers, copied word for word. We never write reviews for you.

02 / How it works

Facts in, post out.

  1. Pick a kind of post

    Deal, event, featured item, announcement, hiring or review. Each one starts with a look that suits it.

  2. Fill in the facts

    Or choose a dish from your menu. Add your logo and one of your own photos. Anything you leave blank stays off the image.

  3. Save and post

    Save it as a square image for your feed or a tall one for stories, then post it from your phone. Duplicate it next week and change the date.
