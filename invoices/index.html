SOGILEX / INVOICES · BETA

Quotes and invoices. Exact to the cent.

Send a quote before the job and an invoice after it, with your logo at the top. Save your customers once. Turn an accepted quote into an invoice in one click, and see at a glance who still owes you. Plain maths, no AI: every number is what you typed.

Make an invoice (free beta) See how it works

01 / Made for

Businesses that bill for their work.

  • Quotes before the job

    Lines, quantities and prices, optional sales tax, and a "valid until" date. When the customer says yes, it becomes an invoice with one click.

  • Invoices after it

    A due date, how to pay you, and a clear total. Mark it paid when the money arrives; the list shows what's unpaid and what's overdue.

  • Monthly plans

    "Bill this again next month" makes next month's invoice from this one. Check it, then send it.

  • Records that stay straight

    Numbers are given in order when you send, with no gaps. A sent invoice can't be quietly changed: cancel it and make a corrected copy, and both stay on your list.

02 / How it works

Lines in, PDF out.

  1. Your details, once

    Business name, address, logo and how customers pay you. They print at the top and bottom of everything you send.

  2. Add the lines

    Pick a saved customer, type what the work was, how many and the price. Totals and tax are worked out to the exact cent as you type.

  3. Send it your way

    Mark it as sent to give it its number, then print it or save a PDF to email. Online payments and emailing from Sogilex are coming later.
