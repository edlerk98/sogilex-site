Privacy policy

What we collect, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. In plain words.

Last updated November 20, 2026.

Who we are

Sogilex (sogilex.com and build.sogilex.com) is run by Keagan Edler, its founder, and Blake Pille, based in St. Louis, Missouri. Sogilex is not yet a registered company. Questions about your information: hello@sogilex.com.

The short version

  • We collect what you give us to make your websites, menus, flyers, posts and invoices, and what we need to run your account.
  • No ads, no tracking or analytics scripts, and we never sell your information.
  • Some features send your words or pictures to an AI service (Anthropic) to do their job. Those features are listed below.
  • Only what you choose to publish is public. Everything else is private to your account.
  • Email us and we'll delete your account and everything in it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored scrambled by our sign-in provider, so nobody, including us, can read them. We also keep the times you sign in.
  • What you make: website descriptions and every saved version of your sites, the changes you ask for, menus, flyers, posts and their captions, quotes and invoices, your business details, your logo and the photos you upload. Photos are resized in your browser before upload, which also removes hidden details such as where a photo was taken.
  • Your customers' details in Invoices: names, emails, phone numbers and addresses you type in. We only store them so you can use them. Only add people you have a reason to bill.
  • Messages to your published site: what a visitor types into your site's contact form. We store it for you, show it in Build and email it to the address you chose. To limit abuse we keep a scrambled code made from the visitor's internet address, not the address itself.
  • Forms on sogilex.com: if you ask for a free sample menu or join the waitlist, what you type there (for example your name, restaurant, email and phone).
  • Usage records: when you build, edit, write a caption or read a menu from a photo, and how much AI processing it used, so we can keep the daily limits and our costs under control.
  • Server logs: the services that host Sogilex keep standard logs, such as internet addresses and times of requests, for security and troubleshooting.

When AI is involved

These features send information to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI, so it can do the work:

  • Build (websites): your description, the changes you ask for, the current page, your logo and small previews of your photos.
  • Reading a menu from a photo: the photos or PDF of your menu.
  • Captions in Posts: the words on the post and any change you ask for.

Menus (apart from reading a photo), Flyers, Posts (apart from captions) and Invoices use no AI. We don't use your content to train AI models. Anthropic handles what we send under its own commercial terms and privacy policy, at anthropic.com/legal.

Who else handles your information

We use these services to run Sogilex. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: sign-in, the database and private file storage.
  • Anthropic: the AI features above.
  • Cloudflare: hosting for published sites and online menus at sogilex.net.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting for sogilex.com and the tool pages.
  • Resend: sending sign-in codes and message emails.
  • Our email provider: receiving mail sent to hello@sogilex.com.

These services may store information in the United States or other countries. We'd also share information if the law required it, for example a valid court order.

What's public

A site you publish at your-name.sogilex.net and an online menu you turn on are public, including the photos and logo on them. Everything else stays private to your account until you print, download, share or send it yourself.

Cookies

We use one sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in across Build, Menus, Flyers, Posts and Invoices, and your browser's storage for the same purpose. No advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and what you've made for as long as your account exists, so you can come back to it. Free sample requests and waitlist sign-ups are kept as long as we need them to reply or follow up, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

  • See or download what you've made in the tools at any time.
  • Delete your account and everything in it: email hello@sogilex.com from your sign-in email address. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Stop message emails with the Stop link in any of them.
  • Questions or corrections: email us.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Each account can reach only its own information, and photos are stored privately. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn that your information was exposed, we'll tell you.

Children

Sogilex is for people 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters for how your information is used, we'll email you first.
