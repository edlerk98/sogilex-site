SOGILEX / MENUS

A menu that looks like your restaurant. Not like a template.

Type your dishes and prices once, pick a look and add your logo. Sogilex sets it to fit the page and print clean. Change a price next month in seconds.

Make your menu (try it free) See how it works

01 / What you get

Set like a real menu.

Dish names you can find at a glance, prices lined up where the eye expects them, descriptions that stay quiet. No clip-art chefs, no fake chalkboard, no stock food photos: only your own.

Anything you don't give us is left off the page. A menu never gets a made-up dish, price or review.

02 / How it works

Three steps, about ten minutes.

  1. Snap or type your menu

    Take a photo of your current menu and it fills itself in, or type sections, dishes, prices, sizes and dietary tags into a simple form.

  2. Pick a look

    One click for a ready-made look (Bold grill, Saloon, Tavern, Retro diner and more), or mix your own: bold colour bands, lettering with character, your logo and your own food photos. Sogilex fits it to the page.

  3. Print it

    Save as a PDF and print it anywhere, in colour or black and white. Come back and change anything, any time.

03 / Our promises

The parts that have to be right.

  • Prices exactly as typed

    Your prices are placed by plain code and never pass through AI, so they can't be changed, rounded or invented. Read from a photo, every price stays highlighted until you've checked it.

  • Updates take seconds

    Change a price, add a special, drop a dish. Your menu updates instantly without a new design.

  • It fits the page

    Letter or A4. Type scales down and switches to two columns to fit one page, and never gets too small to read.

  • Prints clean

    Designed to read just as well on a black-and-white office printer as on good paper.

04 / Questions

Before you ask.

Is it open now?

Yes. Sign in with your Sogilex account at build.sogilex.com/menus and start typing. Your first menu is free to try.

What does it cost?

Try it free. Printed menus come with Basics ($9 a month); your online menu behind a QR code comes with Do it yourself ($19 a month), along with every other tool and your website. See Plans. Nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.

Do I need any design skills?

No. If you can type your dishes and prices, you can make the menu.

Can I add my logo or photos?

Your logo, yes: add it once and it appears at the top of your menu, with a colour palette taken from it. Photos too: your own food photos, one big one at the top and one for any section. They show on the online menu as well. Your own, never stock photos.

Do I have to type my whole menu in?

No. Take a photo of your current menu (or upload a PDF) and Sogilex fills in your dishes, prices and details. Every price it reads is highlighted until you've checked it against your menu, and anything it can't read clearly is marked for you to fill in, never guessed. The photos aren't kept.

Can I put a QR code on it?

Yes. Type any web address (your website, online ordering, reviews or Instagram) and a short line like "Scan to order online", and the code prints at the bottom of your menu.

Is there an online version too?

Yes, with Do it yourself: your menu on its own page made for phones, at your-name.sogilex.net/menu. It updates by itself whenever you change your menu, so the QR code on your tables always shows today's prices.
