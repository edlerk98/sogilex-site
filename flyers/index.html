SOGILEX / FLYERS · BETA

Flyers people actually read.

Your customers can spot an AI flyer from across the room: everything shouting, a waxy crowd, grime on every surface. Sogilex Flyers makes the opposite. One clear message, real typography, and only the facts you give it.

Make a flyer (free beta) See how it works

01 / What we leave out

Everything that makes people scroll past.

  • A wall of calloutsOne headline, one date, one place. A clear first, second and third thing to read.
  • Six fonts at onceTwo typefaces at most. Hierarchy comes from size and space.
  • Generated people and propsNo waxy crowds, no warped hands. Shapes, type, and your own photos.
  • Grime, glow and splatterNo texture to fake energy. Real white space does that better.
  • Made-up detailsNo invented line-ups, prices or quotes. What you don't give us becomes a clear [PLACEHOLDER].

02 / Made for

The flyers a local business actually needs.

  • Events

    Live music, trivia nights, tastings, open houses, grand openings.

  • Sales and offers

    Seasonal sales, specials and promotions, with the deal impossible to miss.

  • Classes and sign-ups

    Workshops, lessons and camps, with the date and how to book up front.

  • Notices

    New hours, a new location, holiday closures. Plain, clear and on brand.

03 / How it works

Facts in, flyer out.

  1. Fill in the facts

    What, when, where, and the one thing people should do. Add your logo if you have one.

  2. Pick a look

    Three layouts, five font pairings and ten colour sets, all built to the rules above. Add one of your own photos if you like. The type sizes itself to fit the page.

  3. Print or post

    Print it or save a PDF in US Letter or A4, or save a square image for Instagram and Facebook.
